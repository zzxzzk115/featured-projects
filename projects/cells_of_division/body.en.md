**Cells of Division** is a top-down bullet hell roguelike developed by **High Path Games**: fight alongside clones of your past runs (up to 6 allies), mix around 100 items, and take down 50+ bosses — destroying viruses to defend the immune system. **Out now on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3879680/Cells_of_Division/)** for Windows, macOS and Linux (released 10 November 2026).
